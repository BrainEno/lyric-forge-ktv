# [2026-11-03 14:33] Put KTV recording back on the fullscreen surface

- Scope: Phase 4.2, third step — the gap left by step 2. Deleting the unreachable `_FullScreenKtvView` removed the only place that could start a take, stop it, export a mix or open the take history; `KtvRecordingService`, `KtvMicrophoneService`, `KtvTakeHistoryStore`, `KtvTakeResultSheet`, `KtvControlLabel` and `recording_time_format.dart` stayed behind with no caller. This change wires them into `ImmersiveKtvScreen` (the product decision recorded in the previous handoff), restores the take-mutation lock, and gives the screen a single nine-collaborator dependency bundle assembled in `lib/app/`.
- Files: `lib/features/player/presentation/playback_screen_dependencies.dart` (new `ImmersiveKtvDependencies`; `PlayerScreenDependencies` drops the two KTV-only repositories in favour of the bundle, `NowPlayingDependencies` gains it), `lib/app/playback_assembly.dart` (`assembleImmersiveKtvDependencies`), `lib/features/player/presentation/screens/immersive_ktv_screen.dart` (one required `dependencies`, recording start/stop, take result sheet, history sheet, microphone sheet, shutdown on dispose, always-visible recording indicator), `lib/features/player/presentation/widgets/ktv_recording_toolbar.dart` (new: record/stop + elapsed time + history + microphone entry points, disabled while exporting), `lib/features/player/presentation/widgets/ktv_microphone_sheet.dart` (new: the deleted `_KtvAudioControlsSheet`, unchanged behaviour), `lib/features/player/presentation/widgets/ktv_take_history_sheet.dart` (new: list/favourite/rename/delete plus the export/recording lock), `lib/features/player/presentation/screens/player_screen.dart` and `lib/features/player/presentation/screens/now_playing_screen.dart` and `lib/features/player/presentation/widgets/now_playing_lyrics_project_action.dart` (pass the bundle instead of five loose services), `test/features/player/presentation/screens/immersive_ktv_recording_test.dart` (new), `test/features/player/presentation/widgets/ktv_take_history_sheet_test.dart` (new), `test/architecture/app_assembly_contract_test.dart`, `docs/reviews/2026-10-31-recognition-quality-and-ux-deep-review.md`, `docs/log/entries/2026-11-03-1433-immersive-ktv-recording.md`, `docs/handoff.md`, `docs/work_log.md`, `docs/work_log_archive.md`.
- Validation:
  - Temporary CI workflow on the pinned toolchain: `flutter analyze --no-pub lib test` clean; the two new widget-test files, the architecture contract and `test/widget_test.dart` green; the repository quality gate green.
  - `test/features/player/presentation/screens/immersive_ktv_recording_test.dart` pumps the real `ImmersiveKtvScreen` with nine fakes and asserts behaviour, not source text: the three entry points exist and are enabled, tapping record starts a take for the loaded project, `isExporting` disables both record and history and opens no sheet, and leaving the screen stops a take that is still running.
  - `test/features/player/presentation/widgets/ktv_take_history_sheet_test.dart` pins the mutation lock: while exporting the favourite action is disabled, tapping a take is refused with `混音正在导出，完成前不能删除、重命名或收藏录音` and the store receives no mutation; while recording the refusal names the recording instead; unlocked favouriting writes through and reloads; the recovery notice is rendered inside the sheet.
  - `test/architecture/app_assembly_contract_test.dart` asserts the KTV studio is assembled from `services.ktvMicrophoneService` / `ktvRecordingService` / `ktvTakeHistoryStore` / `audioPreviewService`, and that the fullscreen screen takes `required this.dependencies` while constructing the toolbar and history sheet.
- Notes: The lock is the part that had to survive verbatim. `_takeMutationLocked` (`isRecording || isExporting`), both refusal sentences, and the double check after the rename dialog and the delete confirmation are all back — the second check matters because an export can start while a dialog is open. Two deliberate differences from the deleted view: the guards now live in `KtvTakeHistorySheet` rather than on the screen, so a widget test can put the recording service into `isExporting` and watch the refusal instead of scanning `player_screen.dart` for a method name; and the "recovered N interrupted takes" message moved from a SnackBar fired before the sheet opened into a banner inside it, because the scan now happens where the list is. The record/stop/export flows still run entirely through `KtvRecordingService` and `AudioPreviewService` — nothing enters the playback queue, so the 4.3 `exclusive_preview` semantics are untouched. The singleton ratchet stays at 54 calls / 29 files: the four KTV services had already left `lib/features` with the dead view, so this step migrates nothing and must not claim a reduction. Two probe rounds were needed because the lock constants were first declared on the private state class (unreachable for the screen) and because a single tap inside `FullscreenKtvInteractionSurface` only resolves after the double-tap timeout — the test now waits for it rather than bypassing the hit test.
