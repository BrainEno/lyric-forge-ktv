- Scope: Phase 4 item 4.3 (review §3.6). The plan said "line audition should go through `AudioPlayerService`; delete the `AudioPlayer` built inside the UI". Reading the code first changed the target: line audition in the lyric editor already went through `PlaybackSessionService` + `AudioPlayerService`, with its own window loop. The player that was out of bounds sat in the KTV take result sheet, and it was audibly worse than the plan described — `stopRecording()` does not pause playback, so pressing 「播放原始人声」 played the fresh take *over* the still-running song, at a different position, with two progress bars that disagreed and no single pause control. - Notes: the design question was whether a take should be played by the app-scoped player. It should not: that player is always "the current queue item", so loading a recording into it would leave the player bar, the lyric editor and the queue describing a song that is no longer audible, and every `seek` in the app would seek inside the recording. A separate `AudioPreviewService` keeps the audition honest, and `ExclusiveAudioPreviewService` enforces the actual invariant — the two channels must not sound at once — in both directions, because the offending widget can disappear mid-flight. Two smaller decisions: auditions never survive the sheet (dismissing stops the take explicitly, since there is no player disposal to fall back on), and the take sheet now checks the recording's existence through an injectable probe, because real file I/O never completes inside the `testWidgets` fake-async zone — without that seam the sheet cannot be tested at all. The user-visible behaviour change is deliberate: previewing a take now pauses the song, and dismissing the sheet leaves it paused rather than silently resuming.
